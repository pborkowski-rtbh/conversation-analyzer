# Funnel: types of problems to identify

Start of the list. Types only, no signals or detection methods yet.

- **Retrieval failure**: the right document exists in the index but was not retrieved.
- **Corpus failure**: the answer is not in the index at all, or the document is stale or contradicts another one.
- **Generation failure**: the right sources were retrieved but the answer misrepresents them.
- **Question failure**: the question is ambiguous or out of scope. Not a system bug, but shows what people expect Bolek to do.
